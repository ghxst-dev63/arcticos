Hand Tracking
New — turn your hand into a cursor

Using your camera and an on-device hand-tracking model, arcticOS can turn your hand into a cursor — pinch to click, drag, and scroll, like on Vision Pro.

To turn it on

Open Settings → Hand Tracking and switch on "Pinch to move windows".

How to use it
  • Pinch your thumb and index finger together to click any button, icon, or app
  • Pinch a window's title bar and move your hand to drag it
  • Pinch the fullscreen, minimize, or close buttons directly
  • Pinch and drag inside a scrollable area to scroll it
  • Pinch twice quickly on the same spot for a right-click
