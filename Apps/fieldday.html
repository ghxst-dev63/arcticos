Field Day

Flying the Timber 1.5 m, 10th Anniversary edition

A short-nosed bush plane, a mown field and a light breeze. The airframe is simulated as a real rigid body: every wing panel, the tail, the prop wash, torque and the sprung tundra gear. Use the flaps for a short take-off, fly a circuit and put it down softly. Hit the trees and it comes apart properly – and if you dive it and yank hard enough, the wings won't stay on.

Controls

Click a slot, then press the key you want. Backspace clears a slot, Esc cancels. Every action has two slots, and your choices are remembered on this device. A gamepad works too: left stick throttle and rudder, right stick elevator and ailerons, A brake, bumpers flaps, Y view.
